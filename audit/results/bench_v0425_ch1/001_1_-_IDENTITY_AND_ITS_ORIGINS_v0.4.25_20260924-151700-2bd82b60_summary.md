## Thorough Summary

The chapter "Identity and Its Origins" addresses the growing clinical interest in identity disturbances among borderline and narcissistic patients, synthesizing scattered literature to explore the concept's developmental origins and theoretical ambiguities. The term "identity was introduced into psychoanalytic literature by Tausk (<em style="font-size:0.9em">1919</em>), who emphasized the lifelong process of self-discovery, while Freud, according to Erikson (<em style="font-size:0.9em">1958</em>), referenced it only once, in his 1926 address to B'nai B'rith.
