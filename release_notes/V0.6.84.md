# V0.6.84 — UI/UX correction Round 4: the compact three-column dashboard at your real window size (3×3 Quick Tools down to the 1100×720 minimum, the three-region sidebar with no internal scrollbar, one theme control, IBM Plex Sans, and the dark-theme hero fix)

> **The correction spec behind this release:** the Round-4 audit
> (`ELIXIR_UI_UX_CORRECTION_ROUND_4.md`) compared the running dashboard
> at **your actual window** (the 1096×717 screenshot) against the approved
> reference and found the remaining gaps were all density gaps. This
> release closes every one of them — measured against the running
> application at that same window, not a mockup.

## What you reported, and what was wrong

| Your report | Root cause | The fix |
|---|---|---|
| "Two columns; only four cards visible" | The Round-3 grid gate demanded ~860 px of grid for three columns — your window offers ~790 | **Floor-driven reflow**: the compact 240 px card floor means three columns need 744 px — the app's own **1100×720 minimum window now shows the full 3×3 grid** (2 columns down to ~492, 1 below) |
| Cards "too large and tall" | The card chrome was calibrated 1:1 against the 1536-wide mockup | **Compact card + density band**: 84–90 px tall at your window (spec's 80–90 band), growing to the reference's ~122 px on large screens, with title/description type scaling 13→17 px along it |
| Sidebar scrolls, last tools clipped | The old rhythm needed ~834 px of rail; your window gives ~633 | **Three regions**: compact primary nav (32 px rows), **Settings/About pinned below the scroll area** (always visible), compact tagline card — the whole rail fits with **no scrollbar** |
| Tagline text clipped | The second line was wider than the compact rail's card | Both brand lines **word-wrap** — "Knowledge, distilled into clarity." is fully readable at every rail width |
| Two theme controls | Moon in the top bar + "Light Theme" in the status bar | **The moon is removed** — the status bar's Light/Dark selector is the single control; the theme engine and persistence are untouched |
| Typeface "looks generic" | Inter | **IBM Plex Sans** (bundled, 4 weights, OFL) — selected over Manrope by rendering BOTH in the real dashboard: better editorial character for the reading-room identity, and narrower metrics that wrap less at compact sizes. Falls back to Segoe UI when absent |
| (Found during verification) Dark mode's hero headline was nearly invisible | `refresh_theme()` never re-styled the hero text — a light-theme ink stayed on the dark hero since V0.6.83 | The hero text column now re-styles on every toggle (pinned by an ink-color test; visible in the new `now5_dark.png`) |

## The hero

The books-and-quill still-life stays a true transparent PNG compositing
over the hero's painted surface in both themes (no rectangular boundary —
verified by pixel-alpha tests). The decorative circle washes step back so
the artwork is the focal point, the hero is proportioned so the full
3×3 grid fits your window without scrolling, and the CTA's arrow no
longer collides with its label at reduced sizes.

## Known remaining differences vs the reference mockup

- The top bar is 50 px (Round-3's spec band) vs the mockup's ~68.
- The hero occupies ~0.33 of the body vs the mockup's ~0.38 — the
  compact-density direction this round's spec asked for.
- Card typography at the reference canvas matches the mockup's scale
  (17/13 px); at your window it is deliberately smaller (13/11 px) so
  nine cards fit — the spec's compact-density trade-off.
