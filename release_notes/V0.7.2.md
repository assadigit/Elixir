# V0.7.2 — Step 4 becomes five tidy category cards with the essentials locked on, honest labels everywhere, professional icons — and the Review + start-log stop silently forgetting nine of your outputs (bugs B8 + B9 closed)

> **What this is:** Phase ④ of the approved wizard rework
> (`ELIXIR_WIZARD_REWORK_ROUND_2_PLAN.md` — Step 4, work items 4a-4d).
> The V0.7.0/V0.7.1 releases rebuilt the wizard shell, Step 1's mode
> cards, and Steps 2-3; this release reworks the step you spend the
> most time on — choosing what Elixir should produce — and fixes the
> two live bugs the audit filed against the output surfaces.

## What you'll notice first

1. **Step 4 stops being a wall of 25 cryptic checkboxes with emoji
   labels.** It becomes a Core card plus five tidy category cards:
   **Audio** (podcast script, TTS audio), **Learning** (flashcards,
   Q&A, study guide, glossary, packets), **Knowledge** (Obsidian KB,
   mind map), **Documents** (DOCX, EPUB), and **Reference** (executive
   briefing, evidence map, compact summaries, simplified keypoints).
   Every card carries an accent-tinted category glyph and a one-line
   caption; every checkbox carries a professional line icon.
2. **The two essentials are locked on, and the label says why.**
   Executive + thorough summaries sit pre-checked and greyed-out under
   the caption **"Always included — every other output builds on
   these."** The master **"Generate summaries"** switch above them
   stays the single off-switch — turning it off cleanly skips every
   summary-dependent output (the audit verified the backend already
   no-ops that way). The lock is structural: the state can never carry
   anything but "on" for the two essentials.
3. **The five deprecated outputs are gone from the wizard** (social
   media pack, patterns, cheatsheet, agent skill, quote bank — D6).
   The generators stay for old files; they can still be enabled via
   Settings. The dead "Streaming LLM output" checkbox is deleted
   outright — it never reached the backend (audit §W8).
4. **The labels finally tell the truth** (§W7: not one tooltip existed
   before). The TTS checkbox now says **"TTS audio — built from the
   executive briefing"** — the generator reads the *briefing* file,
   not the podcast script (bug B9, `podcast_audio.py:108-131`). The
   evidence map admits it **"needs the langextract extra"** and
   degrades to a guidance page without it. Every checkbox carries a
   tooltip that describes what actually gets produced.
5. **The emoji on control labels are gone wizard-wide** (D9): the
   pencil/home/cloud/llama/rocket prefixes on Steps 5-8 became
   professional SVG glyphs — "Refresh models" carries a refresh icon,
   "Test connection" a plug, "Start Conversion" a rocket riding the
   palette's on-accent token — and the Review summary's section
   headers went plain-bold. Emoji survive only in log lines.

## Bug B8 closed — the Review step and the start-log stop forgetting your outputs

**The user-visible symptom:** you selected packets, Q&A, glossary,
EPUB, the evidence map, compact summaries… and the Review step's
outputs line said none of them. The conversion start-log said none of
them either. Nine to ten of the newer outputs were silently omitted
from both surfaces (they still *ran* — you just couldn't see them
listed).

**Root cause (audit §3 B8):** both surfaces hand-enumerated the
output list — 14 `if s.outputs_x: outs.append(...)` lines at
`step8_review.py:156-183` and `wizard_view.py:880-909` — and nobody
updated the lists when new outputs shipped. The audit's exact words:
"drift again."

**The fix is structural, not enumerative:** a new
`core.outputs.registry.selected_output_names()` enumerates the
selected outputs *from the registry* — the single source of truth that
Step 4, the Settings dialog, and the controller already share. Both
surfaces call it. A stub-registry pin test feeds three fake outputs
and asserts every key appears — if anyone ever breaks the wiring, the
test fails. The registry itself grew to cover every wizard output flag
(executive, thorough, and compact summaries joined as first-class
specs), so the enumeration can never drift from what Step 4 offers.
Deprecated outputs never appear (they're not wizard outputs anymore);
summary-children (exec/thorough/keypoints/simplified/compact) are
gated by the master switch, exactly like the backend gates them.

## The registry — quietly the most important change

`OUTPUT_REGISTRY` is now 24 specs (17 studio + 7 core), and every
wizard-selectable output carries its state field, its setting key, a
compact display `short_name`, an honest `subtitle`, and a distinct
SVG `icon_name`. Three consumers read it live: the Step-4 category
cards (labels, icons, state round-trip), the Review line, and the
start-log. Adding an output type is one registry entry plus one
category card line — everything else follows.
