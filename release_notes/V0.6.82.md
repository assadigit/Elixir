## The method: ink-width calibration

Every dashboard font size is re-derived from the reference by rendering
the SAME string side by side and comparing glyph-ink WIDTHS (the pixel
extents of the actual ink). Widths are authoritative; ink heights carry
±2 px of antialias spread, and the V0.6.76 scale had read that spread
as glyph size:

| Token | V0.6.76 | V0.6.82 | Calibration (reference ink vs old render) |
|---|---|---|---|
| tool (card titles) | 15 | 17 | "Summarization" 119 px vs 105 |
| tool_desc | 11.5 | 13 | description line ×1.13 |
| sidebar (nav labels) | 13 | 14.5 | "Full Conversion" 103 vs 94 |
| group (TOOLS label) | 10 | 13 | "TOOLS" 48 vs 35 |
| section ("Quick Tools") | 20 | 20 | 126 ≈ 129 — stays (AA spread) |
| body_lg (hero support) | 22 | 18 | L1 495 = 597×18/22 |
| brand (wordmark) | 44 | 30 | "Elixir" 67 vs 101 (over-sized) |
| leaf mark | 26 | 44 | ink 40×46 vs 23×23 (under-sized) |
| eyebrow tracking | 1.6 | 0.4 | "WELCOME TO ELIXIR" 145 vs 166 |

The **brand row inversion** is the emblematic fix: the wordmark was
1.5× too large while the leaf mark was 2× too small.

## The CTA capsule that renders on EVERY platform

Three independent renderers — this offscreen stack, the V0.6.80 Windows
build (the square CTA visible in the user's `now2.png`), and Qt's
button path generally — paint `QPushButton { background; border;
border-radius }` as a **SQUARE** when the rule lives on the
QApplication stylesheet. Widget-level stylesheets and QFrame rules
round correctly on the same stacks; the app-level button path is the
unstable one (the V0.6.81 1-px border worked around it on Windows but
not everywhere). `_HeroCTA.paintEvent` now paints the capsule directly
(QPainter rounded rect + hover/pressed/disabled states + the 1 px
self-coloured seam) with the QSS rule reduced to
typography/padding/sizeHint. Measured: 249×52 capsule with a true
progressive corner inset, matching the reference's 253×53.

## The retired-grid-column stretch fix (a real reflow bug)

`QGridLayout.takeAt` removes items but never column metadata — after a
reflow 3 → 2 columns, an empty stretch-1 column kept eating up to a
third of the grid: the 2-column layout rendered two squeezed cards
beside a dead column, and the 1-column layout never filled the
viewport. Every column slot up to the largest count ever laid out is
now reset explicitly (the resize sweep caught it — cards rendered at
352 px regardless of the available 534 px).

## The grid matches the reference's measured bands

Cards 116 → 122 minimum (rendered 124 ≈ the reference's rows
[510..634]/[646..770]/[781..912]), gap 15 → 13, heading→grid air
22 → 11, hero→heading 40 → 43. The card's text block is now vertically
centered (the old trailing-only stretch pinned it to the top with ~49
px of dead air below the description). Card icons 26 → 30 px at stroke
2.05; the arrow affordance gains its stem (→, not a bare chevron).

## The artwork is full-bleed

The old `min(max_w, crop.width())` hard-capped the visual at the
504 px native ink crop — always ~40 px short of the reference's 543 px
ink. The budget now governs (540 px at the reference viewport) and the
right margin drops to 0: the still-life reaches the hero's right edge
exactly as the reference paints it.
