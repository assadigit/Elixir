# V0.6.84.1 — the Round-4 polish: single-line sidebar labels, the hero support's pinned two-line break, and the featured card's two-line description

> **What this is:** three small, targeted follow-ups to the V0.6.84
> Round-4 release — each one a wrap defect the spec's acceptance criteria
> call out, found by re-reviewing the shipped build at the user's exact
> field window (1096×717). The V0.6.84 architecture, the IBM Plex Sans
> selection, and every other Round-4 decision are untouched.

## The three fixes (all live-verified at the field window)

| Defect in V0.6.84 | Root cause | The fix | Verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Document → Markdown" wrapped to two lines in the sidebar (spec §3.2: "Prefer the single-line treatment shown in the reference") | the 13 px label measured 141 px against the 133 px label column the 197 px rail offers | `FONT.sidebar` 13 → 12 (the label measures 130 px) + the nav insets tightened (compact margins 8/8→7/6, spacing 10→9; host margins 11→10) — the column is now 139 px at the field window and 130 px at the 188 px rail | `130 ≤ 139` single line, both rails; rendered in light + dark |
| the hero support re-wrapped into a ragged three lines ("…summarized, / organized / and ready…") | the scaled width cap (325 px) sat 6 px under the pinned first line's 13 px advance (331 px), so the V0.6.81 hard two-line newline re-wrapped | the cap is now floored at the pinned line's measured advance (+4 px air) whenever the column has the room | cap 335 ≥ 331 → exactly two lines, both themes |
| the featured card's description wrapped to three lines (spec §2.2: "Descriptions should occupy no more than two lines") | its second line needs 160 px; the 110 px card chrome left 149 | the chrome tightens 110 → 97 (margins 16/12/14/12 → 13/11/10/11, gaps 12/11 → 10/10) + the density band's desc floor 11 → 10 | two lines at 10 px in the 160 px column; card 84 px (spec band 80–90); rows uniform [84, 84, 84] |
