## The hero — measured, not eyeballed

Every position below was scanned from the approved reference at
1536×1024 and then matched in the offscreen render:

| Element | Reference | This build |
|---|---|---|
| Eyebrow band | y 132..142 | y 131..141 |
| Headline line 1 | y 172..201 | y 171..203 |
| Headline line 2 | y 218..256 | y 216..253 |
| Support line 1 / 2 | y 277..294 / 311..330 | y 274..289 / 301..320 |
| CTA capsule | y 349..406 (~258×57) | y 342..395 (~250×54) |

- **Proportions**: wide/shallow (~29% of the content width, clamped
  230–340 px; 340 at the reference viewport), opening flush under the
  top bar exactly as the reference draws it.
- **Typography**: 14 px tracked sage eyebrow; the 41 px semibold
  headline breaking exactly as the reference — a 545 px wrap cap
  guarantees "Turn documents into" / "clear, structured knowledge."
  even when the layout has spare room (a wordWrap label's minimum is
  only its longest word, which previously let the headline render as
  one long line); 22 px support copy.
- **The CTA**: the reference's capsule — play glyph + label + the
  trailing arrow painted INSIDE the pill (`_HeroCTA`, a QPushButton
  subclass; palette-aware, theme-refreshed).
- **The still-life**: the shipped `hero_books_quill.png` carries ~60 px
  of transparent margin that made the books render ~25% small — the
  asset is now cropped to its alpha-ink bbox (cached; the scan is a
  one-off) and scaled near-full-bleed vertically.
- **The decoration**: switched from outline arcs to the reference's
  soft FILLED translucent sage circles plus the understated landscape
  curves along the bottom edge.

## The Quick Tools grid — the reference's exact composition

- **One heading row**: sage zap glyph + "Quick Tools" + "View all
  tools →" at the far right (the old PROCESSING/UTILITIES/AUDIO section
  headers are retired — the reference inserts nothing between the
  heading and the cards). "View all tools →" opens the existing full
  tool dashboard, where the audio tools live on.
- **Nine cards in the reference order**: Summarization, Document →
  Markdown, Translation, Packets of Knowledge, OCR Service, PDF/DOCX
  Cutter, Image Extractor, Markdown Styler, Full Conversion Wizard —
  each the reference's HORIZONTAL card: 26 px sage outline icon left,
  15 px title + 12.5 px description, the pale circular arrow
  affordance at the right. 120 px cards, 15 px gaps, three equal
  columns at wide content (two at medium, one narrow).
- **The ninth card** is the reference's deep forest-green Full
  Conversion Wizard feature card — the correction spec's documented
  override of the old no-duplicate rule: a SECOND ENTRY POINT routing
  through the existing `convert_view` wizard dispatch, never a new
  feature or forked workflow.
- Descriptions wrap (never a single-line minimum — the old cards'
  single-line minimums inflated the whole home view ~300 px past its
  scroll viewport, which is also why the hero headline used to fit on
  one line).
