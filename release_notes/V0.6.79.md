## The Home-pill bug the overlay caught

The pale-sage active pill (`accent_soft`) silently failed to paint in
any real window render — a plain `QWidget` does not paint
app-stylesheet backgrounds without `WA_StyledBackground`, and a direct
widget `grab()` hides exactly that class of bug (the pill rendered
fine in isolation, vanished in the window). Fixed, along with the
missing style repolish on `set_active`.

## Responsive behaviour (spec P2)

- **Hero typography scales with width**: 41/22 px at the reference
  content run down to 55% at the minimum window, with re-capped wrap
  widths — a fixed 41 px headline clipped the word "documents" at the
  560 px minimum, which is now gone. The hero's height floor is 240 px
  so the stacked text + CTA always fit.
- **The top bar yields its labels before it clips**: below ~940 px the
  Settings action drops its word (gear remains); below ~800 px the
  pill drops its name (dot + chevron remain; tooltips carry the full
  status).
- Verified offscreen at **1536×1024 (light + dark), 900×640, and the
  560×400 minimum**: no contrast failures, no clipped controls, the
  CTA visible at minimum, the nav scrolling at short heights.
