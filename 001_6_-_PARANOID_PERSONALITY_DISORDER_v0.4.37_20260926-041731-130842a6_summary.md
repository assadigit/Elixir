- Phobic Character vs. Paranoid Personality: Both display restricted life-styles and avoidance, but the phobic character avoids situations (feeling danger without knowing why) using anxiety and flight, whereas the paranoid personality avoids people (feeling specific personal intentionality) using rage, grudge, and counterattack.
